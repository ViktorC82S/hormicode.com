FAQ

Common questions

Do I have to replace my current system?+

No. Our focus is to repair, improve and modernize what you already have. A rewrite from scratch is expensive and risky; we only recommend it when it truly makes sense.

Can you work on a system someone else built?+

Yes — that's most of our work. We start with a diagnosis to understand the code, the database and the server before making changes.

Will my business have to stop while you work?+

No. Changes are planned and delivered gradually so the operation keeps running.
